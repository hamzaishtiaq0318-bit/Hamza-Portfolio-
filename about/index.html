About me

Hi, I'm Hamza, a full-stack web developer who loves building for the web.

I build websites and web apps for businesses, brands and people with big ideas. Some clients come to me for something custom, built from scratch. Others already have a Shopify, WordPress or Wix site and want it to look better, load faster and sell more. I enjoy both.

My approach is simple: understand what you need, keep you in the loop, and deliver work you're proud to share. I care about the details, like how a page feels on a phone, how fast it loads and how easily your customers find what they're looking for.

How I work

Good websites come from good habits. Here's what you can expect when we work together.

  • Clear communication

    Plain language, regular updates and quick replies. You'll never have to wonder what's happening with your project.

  • Mobile-first

    Most of your visitors are on their phones, so I design for small screens first and scale up from there.

  • Speed matters

    Optimised images, clean code and smart loading, because a slow website loses customers.

  • Built to last

    Tidy, well-organised code and sites you can update yourself, long after I've handed them over.
