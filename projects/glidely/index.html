Overview

Glidely is a team collaboration app I designed and built from the ground up. It brings workspaces, Kanban boards, real-time chat, file sharing and AI-assisted planning into one clean interface, so a team never has to jump between five different tools to get work done.

My focus was speed and clarity. Screens load fast, every interaction feels smooth, and the design system stays consistent whether you're a solo freelancer or a growing team.

What I built

Key features that make Glidely work.

01

Workspaces & boards

Teams create workspaces, organise projects into boards and move cards through a drag-and-drop Kanban flow.

02

Real-time chat

Built-in workspace chat keeps conversations right next to the work instead of in another app.

03

File sharing

Upload and share files with the whole team inside each workspace.

04

AI task planning

Glidely AI helps turn big goals into clear, actionable tasks.

05

Google sign-in & roles

One-click Google sign-in plus role-based access, so the right people see the right things.

06

Always in sync

Every change syncs instantly for everyone in the workspace.
