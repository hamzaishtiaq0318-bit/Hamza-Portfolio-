Overview

Golden Home sells hotel-grade bed linen, towels, bathrobes and bridal bedding. I set up and customised their Shopify theme to give the brand a luxury, hotel-style feel, with big lifestyle photography, a hero slider for each collection and a fast, mobile-friendly checkout.

The store also has the practical details that help it sell: a free-shipping announcement bar, a WhatsApp contact for wholesale orders and clean navigation between men's and women's collections.
