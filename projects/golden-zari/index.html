Overview

Golden Zari by Shujat is a fashion label known for handcrafted bridal couture, formal wedding wear and traditional embroidery. I built their Shopify store to feel as premium as the clothes themselves, with rich full-width imagery, an elegant look and a shopping flow that makes large collections easy to browse.

The theme is customised so the brand's team can update banners, collections and products on their own, without touching any code.

What I built

Key features that make Golden Zari work.

01

Custom theme styling

A refined, editorial look that matches a luxury bridal brand.

02

Organised collections

Clear menus for Bridal Wear, Wedding Wear, Ready to Wear, Sarees and Jewellery.

03

Full-width campaigns

Large banner sections that put the photography front and centre.

04

Wishlist, search & accounts

Shoppers can search, save favourites and manage their orders.

05

Mobile-optimised shopping

Smooth browsing and checkout on phones, where most customers shop.

06

Easy to manage

Everything can be updated from the Shopify admin, with no developer needed.
