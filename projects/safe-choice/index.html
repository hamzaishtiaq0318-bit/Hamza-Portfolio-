Safe Choice

A private, secure health-guidance portal designed so visitors can trust it from the very first second.

Overview

Safe Choice gives people private access to trusted health information and guidance. Because the audience is sensitive, everything about the design is built on trust and discretion: a calm interface, an age-verification step before any content, and a simple way to start a private consultation.

I kept the experience quiet and reassuring, with clear typography, soft colours and no unnecessary steps between a visitor and the help they're looking for.

What I built

Key features that make Safe Choice work.

01

Age verification

A verification step confirms visitors are 18+ before they can view educational content.

02

Private consultations

A simple, discreet flow for starting a one-to-one consultation.

03

Trusted devices

Returning visitors can choose to save their details on a trusted device.

04

Clear, reliable information

Health guidance organised so it's easy to read and easy to find.

05

Discreet interface

A calm, private design that respects a sensitive audience.

06

Works on every screen

Fully responsive, so it's just as easy to use on a phone as on a laptop.
